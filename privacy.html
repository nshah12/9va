Privacy Policy

What we collect through this website, why, and how it's handled.

Last updated: 27 July 2026

Who This Policy Covers

This policy applies to 9va.in ("the Site"), operated by 9VA. It explains what information the Site collects from visitors, how that information is used, and who it is shared with. It does not cover any client data 9VA processes as part of a consulting engagement or product deployment — that is governed separately by the relevant client contract.

Information We Collect

The Site itself does not use cookies, analytics, or visitor tracking of any kind. The only personal information we collect is what you choose to submit through the contact form on the Contact page, namely:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Organization name (optional)
  • Role or title (optional)
  • The nature of your enquiry and any message you write

Fonts used on this Site are loaded from Google Fonts. Loading a font from Google's servers means your browser sends Google your IP address and browser information as part of that standard request, in the same way it would for any externally hosted resource.

How We Use It

Information submitted through the contact form is used only to respond to your enquiry — to route it to the right person at 9VA, reply to you, and keep a record of the conversation. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to any third party.

Third-Party Processing

The contact form is processed by Web3Forms, a third-party form-delivery service, which transmits your submission to 9VA's email inbox. Web3Forms does not require an account from you and acts solely as a delivery mechanism between the form and our inbox. You can review Web3Forms' own privacy practices at their website before submitting the form if you'd like more detail.

How Long We Keep It

Enquiry emails are retained in 9VA's mailbox for as long as reasonably needed to manage the relationship or respond to your request, and are deleted when no longer needed for that purpose.

Your Rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, at any time. To do so, email hello@9va.in and we will act on your request promptly.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information submitted to us, including relying on Web3Forms' transport security for form delivery and restricting access to our mailbox to authorized team members. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

This Site is intended for business audiences and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under the age of 18.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time as the Site or our practices change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent revision.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@9va.in.
