The Business Problem

Powerful technology. Fragmented operations.

Every decade, banks have added another layer — Core Banking, Internet Banking, Mobile Banking, API Banking, Digital Channels, Artificial Intelligence. Each system performs its own function well. Very few understand the complete banking operation.

Replacing the Core Banking System isn't practical. Replacing every surrounding application isn't economical. Adding another point solution only increases complexity. Banking needs an operating layer — not another application.

FAQs

Common questions about ASTRA

Does ASTRA replace our Core Banking System?
No. ASTRA sits above your existing Core Banking System and surrounding applications — it connects, orchestrates and adds intelligence without requiring you to replace decades of technology investment.
What's the first capability we can adopt?
ASTRA CTS — next-generation cheque processing — is the first live capability, with ATM Operations and Reconciliation next on the roadmap.
What deployment models are supported?
Public cloud, private cloud, on-premise, hybrid cloud, sponsor bank model, or a shared banking platform — ASTRA adapts to the bank, not the other way around.
How is AI governed within ASTRA?
Every AI interaction runs through the AI Gateway with authentication, prompt versioning, response logging and human approval where required — every decision is explainable and auditable.
