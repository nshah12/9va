The Business Problem

HRMS manages employment. PRANA manages careers.

An employee's journey is much bigger than joining date, designation, salary and exit date. A career is built through projects, achievements, learning, mentorship, leadership and contributions — and traditional HR systems weren't built to capture any of it.

PRANA transforms exits into long-term connections. Former employees become alumni, mentors, customers, partners and brand ambassadors — the relationship simply evolves.

FAQs

Common questions about PRANA

Does PRANA replace our HRMS?
No. PRANA integrates with your existing HRMS, payroll and learning systems, adding a career-intelligence and alumni layer on top — it manages careers, not employment administration.
What happens to an employee's record after they leave?
Their career timeline, verified achievements and professional network continue — they transition into the Global Alumni Network as mentors, referrers, or future rehires.
Which organizations is PRANA built for?
Enterprises, banks, NBFCs, technology companies, manufacturing, healthcare, universities, government organizations, consulting firms and startups — anywhere people build careers worth preserving.
