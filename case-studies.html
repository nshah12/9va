Different domains, one way of thinking

Banking

Case Study 01 — Reimagining Cheque Processing

Challenge: Cheque processing relied on batch files, manual verification and operational silos, while digital payments evolved dramatically around it.

Our Perspective: Instead of asking "how do we process cheques faster?" we asked "how should cheque operations work if they were designed today?" That question led to ASTRA.

Solution: AI-assisted document intelligence, event-driven processing, an operational command centre and real-time visibility — cheque processing as the first capability, not the final product.

↓ TATReduced Turnaround Time
↑ STPStraight-Through Processing
+ AIAI-assisted Decision Making
B2B Payments

Case Study 02 — BizCollect: Orchestrating Collections

Challenge: Businesses spend enormous effort collecting money — invoices, reminders, follow-ups — while working capital remains blocked.

Our Perspective: Instead of improving collections, what if payment certainty could be established before work even begins?

Solution: Milestone-based agreements, digital acceptance, eNACH integration and AI-assisted commercial intelligence — shifting from chasing payments to orchestrating them.

↑ PredictabilityPayment Certainty
↓ DisputesCommercial Clarity
↑ CapitalWorking Capital Management
Enterprise HR

Case Study 03 — PRANA: From Records to Legacy

Challenge: Organizations invest heavily in developing people, yet experience, knowledge and relationships often disappear when employees leave.

Our Perspective: Employment ends. Professional relationships shouldn't.

Solution: A lifelong career platform combining career documentation, verified achievements, AI-assisted career intelligence and a global alumni network.

↑ RetentionInstitutional Memory
↑ BrandEmployer Reputation
↑ NetworkAlumni Engagement
