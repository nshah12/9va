# Research Themes

Technology evolves every day.

Fundamental business problems do not.

Our research focuses on identifying long-term shifts rather than short-term trends.

We are interested in questions that will remain relevant five years from now.

Not just the technologies that are popular today.

---

# The Questions We Are Exploring

Rather than beginning with solutions,

our research begins with questions.

For example:

- What comes after today's Core Banking Systems?
- Can cheque processing become near real-time?
- What is the future of commercial payments?
- Can lending become continuously intelligent?
- Will AI become enterprise infrastructure?
- How will Digital Public Infrastructure reshape business?
- Can enterprises operate through autonomous workflows?
- What should Banking Platform 2.0 look like?

Every product at 9VA begins as one of these questions.
