Technology is easy. Understanding business is hard.

Organizations rarely fail because they chose the wrong technology. They struggle because technology, business, operations, regulation and execution don't evolve together. At 9VA, consulting begins with the business. Technology follows.

Where We Specialize

Four practices. Clearly defined.

Tech Consulting, IPO Consulting, Regulatory Compliance Consulting and Buyout (M&A) Consulting — four distinct disciplines, each with its own scope. Nothing overlapping, nothing improvised.

Tech Consulting

Strategy, architecture and AI adoption — never technology chosen for its own sake. Every recommendation traces back to a business outcome, applied across banking, lending, payments and enterprise operations.

How We Think

Every recommendation must satisfy five questions

If the answer to any of these is "no," we rethink the solution.

Measurable Value?

Does it create measurable business value?

Simpler Operations?

Does it simplify operations?

Better Experience?

Does it improve customer experience?

Less Complexity?

Does it reduce long-term complexity?

Five-Year Test?

Will it still make sense five years from now?
