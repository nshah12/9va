    Human + AI

    AI amplifies judgement. It doesn't replace accountability.

    Every automated decision moves through rules, AI analysis and — where it matters — a human. Explainable at every step, auditable at every layer.

    AI brings speed and pattern recognition. Humans bring judgement and consequence. The strongest organizations combine both — not substitute one for the other.
