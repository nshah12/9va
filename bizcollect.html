The Business Problem

Invoices record transactions. They don't orchestrate commitments.

Businesses invest heavily in ERP, Accounting, CRM, Banking and Payment Gateways. Yet one question remains unanswered: "When will I actually get paid?" Payments depend on delivery, acceptance, inspection, certification and documents — conditions invoices largely ignore.

BizCollect is not an ERP, not accounting software, not a payment gateway. It's the Commercial Orchestration Layer connecting buyers, sellers, banks and payment infrastructure.

FAQs

Common questions about BizCollect

Does BizCollect replace our ERP or accounting system?
No. BizCollect sits between the front office (CRM/ERP) and back office (accounting, banking, payment rails) as a Commercial Orchestration Layer — it connects and enhances, rather than replaces.
Who can host BizCollect — buyer, seller or bank?
Any of them. BizCollect supports buyer-hosted, seller-hosted, platform-as-a-service, bank-hosted, and industry-network deployment models.
Which industries is BizCollect built for?
Manufacturing, Infrastructure, Construction, EPC, Pharma, Logistics, Distribution, IT Services, Government Procurement and MSMEs — the platform is configurable, not industry-specific.
