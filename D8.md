# Case Study 01

## Reimagining Cheque Processing

### Industry

Banking

---

### Business Challenge

Cheque processing has remained largely unchanged for years.

While digital payments evolved dramatically,

cheque operations continued to rely on:

- Batch Processing
- Manual Verification
- Multiple Independent Applications
- Operational Silos
- Limited Visibility
- High Exception Handling

The objective was not simply to modernize CTS.

It was to rethink cheque operations from first principles.

---

### Our Perspective

Instead of asking,

*"How do we process cheques faster?"*

We asked,

*"How should cheque operations work if they were designed today?"*

That question led to ASTRA.

---

### Solution

A modern Banking Operations Platform built around:

- AI-assisted Document Intelligence
- Event-driven Processing
- Operational Command Centre
- Workflow Orchestration
- Business Rule Engine
- Real-time Visibility
- Sponsor Bank Model
- Open APIs

Cheque Processing became the first capability.

Not the final product.

---

### Business Outcomes

- Reduced operational complexity
- Improved visibility
- AI-assisted decision making
- Platform for future banking capabilities
- Foundation for Banking Platform 2.0

---

# Case Study 02

## BizCollect

### B2B Payment & Collections Orchestration

---

### Business Challenge

Businesses spend enormous effort collecting money.

Invoices are generated.

Reminders are sent.

Collections are followed up.

Working capital remains blocked.

Most collection solutions automate follow-ups.

Very few address the underlying commercial agreement.

---

### Our Perspective

Instead of improving collections,

what if payment certainty could be established before work begins?

---

### Solution

BizCollect enables:

- Milestone-based Agreements
- Digital Acceptance
- Conditional Payment Commitments
- eNACH Integration
- Automated Collections
- Working Capital Visibility
- Bharat Connect Integration
- AI-assisted Commercial Intelligence

The objective shifts from chasing payments...

to orchestrating them.

---

### Business Outcomes

- Improved payment predictability
- Reduced collection effort
- Better buyer-seller trust
- Stronger working capital management
- Financing opportunities through structured payment visibility
